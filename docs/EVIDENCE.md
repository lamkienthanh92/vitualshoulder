## App assumptions (not validated)
- Graft size: the app measures the straight distance from the superior glenoid pole (SG) to the footprint centroid (GT) at the chosen fixation angle, not on a standardised radiograph and not to the footprint's medial edge; it adds 15 mm. The tear (defect) length is typed in by the user.
- Plan comparison: length demand is the change of the shortest path length over the head sphere (glenohumeral motion only, current plane and rotation) relative to the length at fixation; "peak taut strain" is that change divided by the length at fixation. It is a kinematic quantity, not a force or stiffness, and the model's 10–30° slope is about 1.5 times the cadaver value of Mihata 2016, so absolute percentages are probably overestimated.
- "Head elevation left if torn" uses 0 mm for fascia lata and 1.7 mm for dermal allograft (half of 3.4 mm); other techniques have no value.
- Bone clearance: shortest 3D distance between the humeral head/greater tuberosity and the scapular roof (scapular vertices above the superior glenoid pole and more than 18 mm from it); rotation and abduction as in the graft simulation. It ignores cuff remnant, bursa, cartilage and the real thickness of the tensioned graft, and has not been compared with radiographic AHD.
- Fixation-angle matrix and rotation sensitivity are differences of the same simulated path length; they are not forces or tensions.
- Anchor positions of each technique are placed on the template bone by the app from the verbal descriptions above: posterosuperior glenoid rim = 8 mm posterior and 2 mm medial of the superior pole on the glenoid grid; anterior/posterior greater tuberosity = centroids of the anterior/posterior half of the supraspinatus footprint; "middle of the greater tuberosity" = midpoint between the supraspinatus and infraspinatus footprint centroids.
- Biceps loop: the tendon length inside the bone tunnel is not added to the required length.
- Multi-bundle techniques (Box, Snake): required length = sum of the bundle lengths; no isometry map.
- Isometry flag: warn when the current anchors change length more than 3 mm more than the best anchor in the grid (GH 0–60°, relative to the fixation angle).
- Dot colours: tertiles of the current grid.
- LHBT length flag: needs at least graft length at the fixation angle plus 10 mm.
- GH-only motion is used for the isometry map (no scapular rhythm).
